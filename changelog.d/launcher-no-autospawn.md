kind: the launcher no longer lets synapsed-client start a bare synapsed at logon
---
`agentlife launcher` called `synapsed_client::Daemon::connect`, which STARTS `synapsed` when nothing answers. At logon the launcher ran before the daemon's own task, so it auto-spawned a daemon without `SYNAPSE_LAUNCHER_ROLES` and `SYNAPSE_LAUNCH_SENDERS`; the task's own daemon then exited on the held lock, and the launcher was refused on the launch routes (`forbidden: this role may not use the launch routes`, 30 s loop in `launcher.log`, 2026-11-21). The launcher now waits, logging every 30 s, until something accepts a connection at the address `synapsed.json` names, and only then connects; it never starts the daemon. Cause read from the PM's measurement (pid 19312 auto-spawned 04:50:47 by `agentlife.exe launcher`) and from the client source (`connect` spawns once nothing answers). Not run against the real daemon; the manual `--clear` path is unchanged and still may start one.
