kind: each lane's hooks are journaled, and `status` shows how long since they last ran
---
A lane whose hooks stop looked the same as an idle lane: its state file just stayed at its last event (the PM found four working lanes reading `SessionStart` for hours). Every `lane-restart state <event>` now appends one line to `.lane-state/<role>.hooks.log` (`<time> <event> ok|err <ms>ms`; a hook that failed before it knew the role goes to `_unrouted.hooks.log`; 64 KiB, then one rotated generation; a write failure never fails the hook). `status` shows `hook=<N>s` (the journal's age, not moved by `assert-idle` or the launcher) beside `quiet=`, and `activity.last_hook_secs` in `--json`. A lane that is working but whose `hook=` keeps growing has dead hooks; a lane that is idle has both large. Not yet seen against the two stuck lanes: their sessions predate the journal.
